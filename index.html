The sheet is blank. Feed the machine.

What you are watching. A Turing machine writes one cell and moves on. This one cannot. To write cell n it must rewind and read cells 1 through n−1 first, every single time — so each token costs more than the one before it.

Why the KV lamp runs faster. Text you have already inferred stays on the tape and is re-read at 4× under the KV lamp: the machine has seen it, it just has to get past it. Only the run under the Infer lamp is read at full cost. Clear the tape and everything goes cold again.
